# Acceptance tests

Send each message on WhatsApp or the web app.

| # | Message | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Find me flights from Lagos to Abuja next Friday | One reply with options (or a clear "live fares unavailable" + airline sites). No "let me search" message on its own. |
| 2 | What's the weather at Lagos airport right now, will it delay flights? | Quotes the live METAR (time, wind, visibility) in plain words. |
| 3 | What is Air Peace's checked baggage allowance and refund policy? | Quotes Air Peace's own page with the URL, not "general information". |
| 4 | Do Nigerians need a visa for the UK? What does it cost? | Links gov.uk, says to confirm with the embassy, any fee is quoted from the source. |
| 5 | Plan 4 days in Accra from 2026-12-12 with a budget of 500000 naira | Day list matches dates and weekdays; table adds up; within budget; amounts in GHS and NGN with rate date. |
| 6 | Hello (send twice, a minute apart) | First reply introduces Airxpact; second is one short line. |
| 7 | What currency do they use in Morocco and how much is 100000 naira? | Moroccan dirham (MAD), live converted amount and rate date. |
| 8 | Bonjour, je vais à Lagos la semaine prochaine. Que dois-je savoir ? | Whole reply in French. |
| 9 | I'm going to Lomé, I only speak English | Offers French phrase sheet with pronunciation and translators/interpreters in Lomé. |
