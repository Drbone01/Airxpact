# Airport Day Guide

## When to arrive
- International flights: arrive 3 hours before departure. Domestic: 2 hours. Busy hubs or peak holiday dates: add 30-60 minutes.
- Online check-in usually opens 24-48 hours before departure and closes 60-90 minutes before.
- Bag drop typically closes 60 minutes before international and 45 minutes before domestic flights (check your airline).
- Gates usually close 15-20 minutes before departure.

## Steps
1. Check-in (online or counter) and bag drop.
2. Security screening: liquids in containers of 100 ml or less in one clear resealable bag (about 1 litre) at most airports, unless the airport uses new scanners that allow larger liquids. Laptops may need to come out.
3. Emigration / passport control for international flights.
4. Go to the gate; boarding starts about 30-45 minutes before departure.

## Connections
- Check whether your bags are checked through to the final destination.
- If both flights are on one ticket the airline must rebook you if you miss the connection due to a delay.
- Transit may require a transit visa depending on your nationality and the country; always confirm with the embassy or the airline.

## Documents
- Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond travel is required by many countries.
- Visa or electronic travel authorisation where needed (for example UK ETA, US ESTA, Schengen visa).
- Keep printed or offline copies of booking reference, e-ticket and hotel address.
